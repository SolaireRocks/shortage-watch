Current Supply Context

National pharmaceutical supply conditions remain in a stable posture. The Pharmaceutical Scarcity Index sits at 39.8, resting comfortably within the normal range. The FDA currently tracks 69 active shortages, with only two additions over the trailing 90 days (Olipudase alfa-rpcp Injection and Pentostatin Injection). Forecast and What to Watch

Probabilities for broad pharmaceutical disruption remain near historical base rates across all horizons (0.4% at 1 week; 5% at 3 months; 21% at 1 year).
