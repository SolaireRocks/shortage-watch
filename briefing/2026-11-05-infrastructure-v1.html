Current Infrastructure Posture

The infrastructure risk index sits at 70.6, holding in the elevated band. The principal pressures continue to stem from digital threat surfaces and a cluster of global seismic events. CISA reports 34 actively exploited vulnerabilities added within the last 30 days, including newly patched zero-days affecting Ivanti Connect Secure appliances. In parallel, European grocers experienced a second wave of cyber intrusions tampering with supermarket refrigeration systems, demonstrating adversary capability against physical cooling controls. However, no core US bulk infrastructure—such as regional power grids, major pipelines, or port logistics—has suffered an operational outage.

Assessing the Risk

Historically, events causing multi-day shutdowns of critical US infrastructure (such as the 2021 Colonial Pipeline ransomware incident, Winter Storm Uri, or the 2024 Change Healthcare outage) occur roughly once every four to five years, yielding a baseline weekly probability of approximately 0.4% and an annual probability of 20%.

We price the near-term risk modestly above historical base rates—at 1.5% for one week and 5.0% for one month—reflecting elevated edge-device exploitation and active targeting of operational technology. Space weather remains quiet, with the planetary Kp index at a low 3.33, well below levels that stress transmission grids. Because recent attacks remain concentrated in corporate networks and international retail cooling rather than North American bulk infrastructure, our baseline probabilities remain unchanged from yesterday.

What We Are Watching

We monitor CISA alerts for exploits targeting industrial control systems or key administrative backbones in logistics and healthcare. Additionally, we track NOAA Space Weather forecasts for sudden coronal mass ejections and early winter weather forecasts that could challenge regional electrical interconnections.

What would change this view

  • Confirmed exploitation of critical industrial control or supervisory systems operating a major US pipeline, bulk power interconnect, or primary port operator.
  • A major space weather event (Kp index reaching 8+ / G4 geomagnetic storm) or severe physical winter weather pattern directly threatening regional grid reliability.
