Current Status

Novel pandemic probabilities remain firmly tied to historical base rates across all forecast windows: 0.1% for 1 week, 0.4% for 1 month, 1.3% for 3 months, 2.6% for 6 months, and 5.5% for 1 year. What to Watch

Over the next few weeks, surveillance will center on the trajectory of domestic respiratory illness indicators—including emergency department percentage shares and wastewater viral loads—to gauge the velocity of standard seasonal waves. Household focus should remain on routine seasonal protections rather than novel pathogen disruption, while monitoring veterinary and public health reports for any unexpected shifts in avian influenza transmission biology.

  • A sharp, unexplained escalation in domestic intensive care unit admissions or severe respiratory clusters deviating from seasonal norms.
