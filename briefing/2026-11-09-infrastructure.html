Cyber risk indicators account for the primary source of mild background friction. Thirty actively exploited vulnerabilities were cataloged by CISA over the past 30 days, including one targeting industrial control systems. Our forecast assesses the probability of a major event—defined as a 3+ day shutdown of a primary pipeline, top-10 port, regional electrical grid, or national pharmacy clearinghouse—at 1.5% over the next week and 5.0% over the next month, rising to 26% over a full one-year horizon. These estimates remain slightly above historical base rates (which average roughly one major incident every four to five years) to account for elevated operational cyber threats against commercial and utility control networks, but they do not warrant panic or departure from baseline readiness. With no acute operational outages detected, no emergency household actions are required today beyond maintaining standard home emergency supplies.
