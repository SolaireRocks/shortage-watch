Current Supply Assessment

Broad national pharmaceutical supply conditions remain stable. The Pharmaceutical Scarcity Index (PSI) sits at 34.0, comfortably within the normal historical range. The American Society of Health-System Pharmacists (ASHP) reported 225 active drug shortages at the close of Q3 2026, remaining well below the 2024 peak of 323 and the qualifying disruption threshold of 300. In addition, the FDA tracks 68 active shortages, with only one new drug (Pentostatin Injection) added in the past 90 days. Generic price shock breadth stands at a negligible 0.8%, confirming that wholesale disruption across essential oral generic classes is currently absent.

What We Are Monitoring

Over the coming weeks, attention focuses on Federal Register notices concerning DEA telemedicine rules. Households utilizing remote prescribing should verify their clinic's contingency plans for in-person evaluations prior to year-end.

What would change this view

  • A DEA Federal Register publication extending Schedule II telemedicine prescribing flexibilities or establishing a permanent special registration rule before December 31, 2026.
  • ASHP active drug shortages reversing their current downward trend and climbing back above 250 toward historical peaks.
