Probability Rationale

Forecasted probabilities for a pandemic-level event remain anchored near historical base rates, tracking essentially flat from recent readings (0.1% at 1 week up to 5.5% at 1 year). The base rate reflects historical precedent—roughly one novel global respiratory emergency every two decades, as seen with 2009 H1N1 and 2020 COVID-19. What We Are Watching

We monitor genomic surveillance of influenza strains, wastewater pathogen tracking, and CDC emergency room trends for any sudden deviations from normal seasonal curves. Particular attention is maintained on any reports of mammalian adaptation or household clustering of avian influenza.

What would change this view

  • A World Health Organization declaration of a Public Health Emergency of International Concern (PHEIC) for a novel respiratory pathogen.
  • A rapid, unseasonal surge in emergency department visits or wastewater signals indicating an uncharacterized pathogen.
