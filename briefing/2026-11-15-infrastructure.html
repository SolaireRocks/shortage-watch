Current Conditions and Trajectory

The aggregate infrastructure risk index sits at 54.4, squarely within the normal band and down 10.2 points over the past week. No active disruptions are currently affecting major US physical networks, energy corridors, or freight hubs.

Cybersecurity indicators represent the primary source of mild background stress. CISA recorded 33 newly exploited vulnerabilities over the trailing 30-day period (stress score 1.57), alongside one industrial control system vulnerability identified over the last 90 days. Additionally, reporting from The Record noted an isolated cyber incident against a legacy system at shipping giant Maersk. While maritime and logistics networks remain frequent targets of reconnaissance and extortion attempts, there is no evidence of sustained service halts at major domestic ports or distribution pipelines.

Physical and space weather indicators remain placid. Daily maximum geomagnetic activity registered at Kp 2.0, well below threshold levels that threaten power distribution grids, while global seismic and hazard readings match long-term historical medians.

Why These Probabilities

Historically, severe infrastructure outages lasting three days or longer—such as the 2021 Colonial Pipeline disruption, Winter Storm Uri, or the 2024 Change Healthcare outage—occur roughly once every four to five years. This establishes a baseline annualized probability of approximately 20% (0.0043 per week, 0.0182 per month).

We assess current odds somewhat above pure historical base rates across all horizons (1.5% at one week, 5.0% at one month, and 26.0% at one year) to account for persistent cyber scanning of critical supply chain nodes and health networks. However, without active operational paralysis or severe severe weather events underway, the near-term risk of a multi-day systemic failure remains low.

What We Are Monitoring

We continue to track operational status reports across pipeline corridors, East and West Coast container terminal dwell times, space weather forecasts from NOAA SWPC, and vulnerability advisories concerning industrial control platforms.

What would change this view

  • A confirmed physical outage or cyber shutdown at a top-10 US container port or key refining pipeline.
  • A severe geomagnetic storm (Kp 8 or higher) threatening regional high-voltage transmission equipment.
  • Widespread operational disruption to clearinghouses or pharmacy claims processors resembling the 2024 Change Healthcare outage.
