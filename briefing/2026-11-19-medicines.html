Current Supply Conditions

Overall pharmaceutical availability across the United States remains in normal territory. The Pharmaceutical Scarcity Index (PSI) registers at 39.2, well below elevated thresholds. The American Society of Health-System Pharmacists (ASHP) reported 225 active drug shortages at the close of Q3 2026, remaining well beneath the 2024 peak of 323 and our 300-shortage event threshold. FDA active shortages stand at 69, with generic price shock breadth subdued at 0.6%.

What We Are Watching

  • Federal Register filings: Watching for DEA or HHS notices granting a fifth temporary extension or final special registration rule for telemedicine prescribing ahead of December 31.
  • ASHP quarterly tallies: Tracking whether persistent hospital and injectable shortages begin trending back toward historical highs.

What would change this view

  • ASHP active shortages rebounding above 300 from the current 225 level.
