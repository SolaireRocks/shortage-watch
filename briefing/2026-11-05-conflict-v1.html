Current Geopolitical Picture

Chokepoint Dynamics and Forecasts

Maritime operations in the Persian Gulf remain severely constrained. Prediction markets reflect substantial skepticism that transit volumes will normalize quickly, with Polymarket pricing just a 4% probability that the Strait of Hormuz resumes normal traffic (exceeding 60 transits per day on a 7-day moving average) before the end of November. We align our 1-month reopening probability with this market consensus at 4.0%, while anticipating eventual resolution or adaptive escort arrangements over longer horizons (35% over six months and 55% within a year).

Household Perspective

Because the conflict index is in the normal range and no immediate escalation beyond active combat zones is evidenced in the hard data, no special household preparedness actions are warranted today. We continue to monitor IMF PortWatch transit reports and satellite tracking around energy infrastructure for early indications of shifts in maritime access or regional spillover.
