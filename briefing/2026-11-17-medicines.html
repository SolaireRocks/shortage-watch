Supply Landscape

Overall drug availability remains in a calm band across the United States. The Pharmaceutical Scarcity Index stands at 17.4, well within the normal historical range. The American Society of Health-System Pharmacists (ASHP) tracked 225 active drug shortages through the end of the third quarter of 2026, substantially beneath the early-2024 peak of 323 and our 300-shortage disruption threshold. The FDA currently lists 69 active shortages, with recent additions limited to niche specialty injectables (olipudase alfa-rpcp and pentostatin). Generic price-shock breadth is negligible at 0.8%.

What to Watch

Over the next six weeks, monitor the Federal Register for DEA action regarding an extension or permanent framework for telemedicine prescribing. For general medicines, watch quarterly ASHP shortage totals and FDA generic recall trends.

What would change this view

  • A sudden surge in ASHP active shortages back toward 300, or a widespread manufacturing disruption affecting a major oral antibiotic class, would sharply increase the general pharma forecast.
