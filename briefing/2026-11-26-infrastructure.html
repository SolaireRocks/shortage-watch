Forecast Justification

The target event requires an outage halting a major US pipeline, a top-10 port, a regional electric grid, or a national pharmacy claims clearinghouse for at least 3 days. Historically, such events occur roughly once every four to five years (Colonial Pipeline, Winter Storm Uri, Change Healthcare). Current probabilities are held moderately above pure historical base rates (1.5% at 1 week, 5.0% at 1 month, and 26.0% at 1 year) to account for persistent, high-tempo cyber exploitation attempts and supply chain friction, though absence of active systemic outages prevents higher escalations.

What would change this view

  • An active cyber intrusion resulting in sustained operational shutdown of a major US fuel pipeline, regional electric transmission interconnect, or primary healthcare claims switchboard.
  • Severe space weather reaching G4 or G5 intensity (Kp >= 8) with geomagnetically induced currents threatening high-voltage transformer substations.
