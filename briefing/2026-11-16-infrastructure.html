Overview and Current Conditions

The composite infrastructure risk index sits at 50.3, squarely in the normal band, reflecting a period of quiet across major physical and geophysical systems. Over the past month, the index has eased roughly 14 points as natural hazard threats remain low. The Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System (GDACS) reports zero orange or red disaster alerts currently in force, and geomagnetic activity is quiet with a peak daily planetary K index of 0.67 (well below the Kp 8 threshold that would signal severe power grid risk).

Cyber Activity and Physical Infrastructure

Digital threats remain the primary stressor on critical infrastructure systems. CISA added 33 actively exploited vulnerabilities to its catalog over the past 30 days (stress score 1.57), alongside one exploited vulnerability in industrial control systems over the past 90 days. While shipping giant Maersk reported a cyber incident affecting a legacy platform, operations have avoided the severe multi-day systemic outages that define our infrastructure disruption threshold (such as a 3-day complete halt at a top-10 port, regional electrical grid, or major fuel pipeline).

Forecast Outlook

Historical base rates suggest a major national infrastructure disruption occurs roughly once every 4 to 5 years (annual probability of ~20%, with 1-month baseline odds near 1.8%). Because active exploitation of enterprise software remains elevated and global logistics networks face persistent probing, we maintain a slight elevation above historical baseline odds across all horizons (1.5% at 1 week, 5% at 1 month, and 26% over 1 year). We will monitor CISA operational advisories, industrial control telemetry, and seasonal weather patterns for changes that could threaten regional lifeline networks.

What would change this view

  • A confirmed cyber intrusion forcing operational shutdowns of key pipeline distribution systems, major port terminal operations, or national clearinghouses.
  • Severe space weather reaching Kp 8+ (G4-class geomagnetic storm) threatening power transmission networks.
  • A major hurricane, seismic event, or winter storm causing multi-day regional grid collapse.
