Current Infrastructure Posture

The composite infrastructure risk index sits in the elevated band at 68.8, down slightly by 0.9 points over the last seven days but up 14.1 points over the last month. Elevated risk continues to be driven primarily by the cyber threat environment rather than acute physical or space weather hazards. The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) added 34 newly exploited vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog over the trailing 30-day window, representing a stress score of 1.69 standard deviations above average.

Recent reporting also illustrates continued cyber targeting across critical supply chain nodes. Shipping carrier Maersk acknowledged a recent cyber incident impacting a legacy system, and US law enforcement extradited suspects linked to ransomware campaigns targeting healthcare entities. However, none of these disruptions meet the threshold of knocking a top-10 port, regional electrical grid, major pipeline, or national pharmacy claims network offline for three or more consecutive days.

Why Probabilities Remain Stable

Historically, events causing three-day outages across critical US national infrastructure occur roughly once every four to five years (such as Winter Storm Uri and the Colonial Pipeline ransomware attack in 2021, and the Change Healthcare disruption in 2024). Base rate probabilities sit at 0.4% weekly, 1.8% monthly, and 20% annually.

Because active vulnerability exploitation remains higher than baseline, near-term probabilities are held at 1.5% over one week and 5.0% over one month, scaling to 26% over a full year. Physical hazards remain benign: the planetary K-index stands at 3.33 (well below the threshold of 8 indicative of a severe G4 geomagnetic storm), and global seismic metrics are near baseline. With no active critical outages reported, no emergency household actions are required today beyond routine preparedness.

What would change this view

  • A confirmed zero-day exploit impacting widespread industrial control systems or operational technology in pipeline or power grid networks.
  • A severe geomagnetic storm (Kp 8 or higher) or an extreme winter storm threatening multiday regional grid reliability.
  • A cyber incident causing confirmed multiday operational halts at a top-10 container port or critical pharmaceutical clearinghouse.
