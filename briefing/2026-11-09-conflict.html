Overview

The Conflict and Geopolitics Index sits at 47.3, firmly within the normal band. The global conflict death tally from UCDP stood at 13,286 for September, carrying a stress score of -0.07. Chokepoints and Reopening Prospects

The primary focus remains maritime transit through the Strait of Hormuz. According to IMF PortWatch data, transits recorded just 1.0 ship per day on November 1, bringing the 7-day average to 4.4 ships per day. Because resolution of the reopening metric requires the 7-day average to climb back above 60 transits daily, maritime flow remains severely constrained. Crowd sentiment aligns closely with this depressed baseline: Polymarket prices a return to normal traffic by November 30 at just 2.4%. Consequently, our near-term probability of reopening remains low at 1.0% over one week and 3.0% over one month, scaling to 53% by the one-year mark.

Commodity Conflict Shocks

A conflict shock requires a new war or blockade involving a top-five global exporter of oil, natural gas, fertilizer, or grain (or a blockade of Taiwan), rather than the continuation of existing hostilities. Because major baseline shocks historically occur roughly once per decade, and absent concrete signs of a new front opening against top exporters, we hold the near-term risk low at 0.2% over one week and 0.9% over one month, rising to 11% over the full year.

What to Watch

Key indicators to track include updates to IMF PortWatch transit statistics through the Strait of Hormuz, commercial maritime rerouting patterns, and any emerging diplomatic signals or naval escort protocols that could alter transit security.

What would change this view

  • Sustained increases in PortWatch daily transit counts through the Strait of Hormuz rising towards 20 to 30 ships per day would sharply boost near-term reopening odds.
  • New military actions threatening commercial shipping or energy infrastructure outside the current Iran conflict zone, such as near the Taiwan Strait or Baltic pipelines, would elevate conflict shock probabilities.
