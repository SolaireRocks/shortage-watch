Current Supply Conditions

Broader pharmaceutical supply remains in stable condition. The Pharmaceutical Scarcity Index (PSI) registers at 29.2, holding in the normal band following an 8.2-point decline over the past 30 days. According to the American Society of Health-System Pharmacists (ASHP), active drug shortages totaled 225 at the close of Q3 2026, down significantly from the peak of 323 recorded in early 2024. The FDA lists 69 active shortages, with only one new chemical entity (Olipudase alfa-rpcp injection) added within the past 90 days. Furthermore, generic price shock breadth remains minimal at 0.6%.
