Current Situation

The geopolitical risk index stands at 47.3, firmly within the normal historical band. While global conflict metrics remain steady—Uppsala Conflict Data Program (UCDP) figures recorded 13,286 battle-related deaths worldwide in September with a neutral stress score of -0.07—maritime movement through the Persian Gulf remains severely suppressed. This remains far below the historical benchmark of 60 transits per day required to declare the waterway reopened.

Assessment & Horizon Probabilities

We maintain low near-term probabilities for a resolution of the maritime choke point. For a new conflict_shock—defined as a distinct new war or blockade involving a top-five exporter of energy, fertilizer, or grain—we hold near base rates at 0.2% for 1 week and 0.9% for 1 month, drifting to 11.0% across 1 year. What to Watch

Key indicators to monitor over the coming week include daily AIS transit counts from IMF PortWatch, potential diplomatic announcements regarding safe-transit corridors in the Gulf, and any military posture changes around key export choke points such as the Taiwan Strait.

  • Emergence of maritime security escorts or formal diplomatic ceasefires restoring commercial shipping passage.
  • New military actions or blockades targeting critical agricultural or energy choke points in the Taiwan Strait or Baltic Sea.
