Current Supply Conditions

National pharmaceutical availability remains in a manageable posture overall. The Pharmaceutical Scarcity Index sits at 38.9, firmly within the normal range. According to the latest third-quarter reporting from the American Society of Health-System Pharmacists (ASHP), active drug shortages total 225. While hospital systems still face localized constraints in chemotherapy agents, injectable contrast media, and select controlled substances, total active shortages remain well beneath the peak of 323 reached in early 2024. FDA-tracked active shortages stand at 69, with only two specialized injectable additions in the trailing 90 days.

Household Guidance

Maintain standard prescription refill buffers. Patients receiving Schedule II maintenance medications through telemedicine should confirm their clinic's year-end protocol or schedule an in-person baseline visit before December 31 to prevent any administrative interruption in care.
