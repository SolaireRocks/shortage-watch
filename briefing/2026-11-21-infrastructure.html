Current Infrastructure Posture

The infrastructure and natural hazards composite index sits at 64.5, maintaining an elevated posture after a 5.0-point weekly rise. Physical and Space Hazards

Physical and space weather indicators remain quiescent. Probability Outlook

Historically, severe systemic infrastructure failures—such as the 2021 Colonial Pipeline shutdown, Winter Storm Uri, or the 2024 Change Healthcare outage—occur roughly once every 4 to 5 years (a 20% annual base rate). Current elevated software vulnerability pressure and active targeting keep the 1-week probability at 1.5% and the 1-month probability at 5.0%, slightly above historical base rates (0.43% and 1.82%, respectively). In the absence of an imminent severe weather system or an active operational shutdown, no extraordinary household actions are recommended beyond routine preparedness.

What would change this view

  • A confirmed zero-day exploit crippling operational technology across a major domestic power grid or fuel pipeline.
  • A severe geomagnetic storm (Kp 8 or higher) threatening regional high-voltage transmission networks.
  • A major Category 4+ hurricane or severe winter storm knocking out regional power or port infrastructure for multiple consecutive days.
